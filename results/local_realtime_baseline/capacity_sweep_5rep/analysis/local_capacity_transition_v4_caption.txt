Local capacity characterization with B=1, C=1, 30 frames/s per stream and 1,800 frames per stream, using five separate repeated runs per K. (a) Arithmetic mean of run-mean local E2E latency for K1-K6; shading indicates the run min-max range. (b) Arithmetic mean of five per-run backlog growth rates for K1-K7; no variability markers or error bars are shown. Each rate is the ordinary least-squares slope of one-second time-weighted backlog bins over [10,59) seconds relative to the first actual arrival. The horizontal reference denotes zero growth. (c) K6 and K7 time-weighted backlog trajectories; thin lines denote individual runs, thick lines denote five-run means, and the inset provides a K6 detail view. Backlog uses actual-arrival (+1) and completion (-1) events, with arrivals first on exact timestamp ties. Trajectories end at the earliest last actual arrival among these ten runs; their final partial bin uses its actual duration. The replicate unit is the run, not the frame. Historical and long-duration results are excluded.
